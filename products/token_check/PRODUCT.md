# Token check

**Kind:** data_feed (interactive)
**Built from:** the public record (feed/, ledger/warnings, ledger/prices, the census) as of 2026-10-13
**Demand:** 7 tokens recorded as sell-blocked with 35 ETH of buyers' money in their pools; ENDAI's
trapped sellers rose from 18 to 47 in a week; 3 imitation stock tokens (MCD, LULU, GPRO); 67 tokens
named like one agent project alone. People are buying these with no way to check first.
**Coverage today:** none found on this chain; the explorer shows contract data but not whether a
token can be sold or whether it copies a registry token.
**What it is:** message the Witness Telegram bot a token address or ticker; it replies with what the
record shows: registry or imitation, sell-blocked or not, Witness's price and how solid it is, and
for stock tokens the feed, depth, pause and halt history and the safe loan-to-value tier.
**Will not do:** recommend buying or selling; read anything but the published record; act on what
a message says. Input is matched against two patterns and otherwise ignored.
**Nearest rail:** never recommend a trade to a person; published figures are measurements.
